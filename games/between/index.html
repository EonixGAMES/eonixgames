About the gameعن اللعبة

BETWEEN is an atmospheric fantasy adventure game developed by EONIX GAMES.

Enter a mysterious reality shaped by two interconnected worlds: AETHER and UMBRA.

Each world presents a different side of this mysterious universe, revealing contrasting environments, hidden secrets, and unexplored places.

Follow the journey of THE TRAVELER as the boundaries between these realities become part of an extraordinary adventure.

Discover what lies BETWEEN.

Two Worlds. One Journey.

Currently in development by EONIX GAMES.

BETWEEN هي لعبة مغامرات فانتازية ذات أجواء غامضة، من تطوير EONIX GAMES.

ادخل إلى واقع غامض يتشكل من عالمين مترابطين: AETHER وUMBRA.

رافق THE TRAVELER في رحلته، حيث تصبح الحدود بين هذين الواقعين جزءاً من مغامرة استثنائية.
