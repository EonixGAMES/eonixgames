Development of BETWEEN Has Officially Begunانطلاق عملية تطوير لعبة BETWEEN رسمياً

12 November 202612 نوفمبر 2026

EONIX GAMES officially announces the beginning of development of its first game, BETWEEN, an atmospheric fantasy adventure set between two interconnected worlds: AETHER and UMBRA.تعلن EONIX GAMES رسمياً عن بدء تطوير أول ألعابها BETWEEN، وهي مغامرة فانتازية غامضة تدور بين عالمين مترابطين: AETHER وUMBRA.

A New Journey Begins.

EONIX GAMES is excited to announce that development of BETWEEN, the studio's first official game, has officially begun.

BETWEEN is an atmospheric fantasy adventure built around two interconnected worlds: AETHER and UMBRA.

Players will follow THE TRAVELER through mysterious environments, discovering the differences and connections between these two realities.

This marks an important milestone for EONIX GAMES as the studio begins its journey into game development.

BETWEEN is currently in development.

More details and development updates will be shared in the future.

سيرافق اللاعب THE TRAVELER في رحلته عبر بيئات غامضة، لاكتشاف الاختلافات والروابط بين هذين العالمين.
